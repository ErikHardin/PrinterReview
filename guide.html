Everything you need to know to complete your printer review

Getting Started
1
Open the app
Go to ech-technicalsolutions.com/PrinterReview in Safari. For the best experience, tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen so it opens like a native app.
3
Enter your PIN
Your PIN is the last 4 digits of your phone number. The app will recognise your name automatically. You'll be asked for your PIN every time you open the app.

Want a different PIN? Tap the 🔑 key icon in the header after logging in to change it anytime.
4
Select your building and floor
Use the building dropdown to select your assigned building, then tap a floor chip to filter to your floor. Printers are sorted by room number.
5
Review each printer
Tap a printer to open the decision screen. Make your decision and tap Save Decision. The row will turn green, red, or gray depending on your choice.
Your Three Decisions
✅
Keep It
Printer stays in service. Row turns green.
🗑️
Retire It
Select a replacement printer or check Secure Print. Row turns red.
🔍
Not Found
Printer couldn't be located physically. Row turns gray.
Quick Actions
👆
Swipe right on a pending printer to instantly mark it as Keep — no need to open it. Fast for obvious decisions.
↩️
Swipe right on a printer you already decided to reset it back to Pending. Only works on your own decisions.
🔍
Use the search bar to find a printer by D#, B#, Serial Number, model, room, or department.
⏳
Tap Pending in the Status filter to see only printers that haven't been reviewed yet — great for tracking what's left.
Status Badges
Pending ✓ Keep ✗ Retire ? Not Found ⚠ Conflict
A Conflict appears when two reviewers make different decisions on the same printer. The coordinator will resolve these. You can filter to conflicts using the status chips.

Good to Know
🟢
Everything syncs live. You can see other reviewers' names and locations at the top of the screen in real time.
📋
Lease dates shown in color. Orange = expiring within 180 days. Red = expiring within 90 days or already expired.
⚠️
High volume printers (500+ pages/month) are flagged with a ⚠️ in the list to help prioritize decisions.
📶
No signal? Keep reviewing. Decisions save locally and sync automatically when you reconnect.
🎉
Floor complete! When every printer on a floor is reviewed, a celebration screen appears. Tap Continue to move on.
Review Schedule
📅
Tap the 📅 button in the header to open the review schedule. It shows all floors assigned to both teams for the day with time slots and department partners.
🔵
Filter by Team A or Team B using the chips at the top. By default it shows today's schedule only — tap All Dates to see the full schedule across all days.
📊
Progress bars update live on each floor card as decisions are made — so you can see at a glance how far along each floor is without leaving the schedule view.
→
Tap "Go to this floor" on any floor card to jump directly to that building and floor in the printer list.
